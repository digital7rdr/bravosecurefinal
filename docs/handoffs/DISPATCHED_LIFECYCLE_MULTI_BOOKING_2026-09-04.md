## 1. The lifecycle now

```
booking  PENDING_OPS → OPS_APPROVED → DISPATCHING → CONFIRMED ─────────────────────────────→ COMPLETED
                                          │              │ (accept = escrow HELD, crew SLA 15 min)
                                          │              ▼
mission                                   │        CREWED ──(Dispatched: agency or lead)──→ DISPATCHED → PICKUP → LIVE → COMPLETED
                                          │        "team assigned"                            "team      "team    "client received ·
                                          │        NOT dispatched                              dispatched" arrived"  service started"
customer  finding_provider ────────────→ provider_accepted → team_assigned → team_dispatched → team_arrived → service_started → completed
```

- `CREWED → PICKUP` is impossible (TS FSM + DB trigger + drift spec): a crew cannot arrive before it was dispatched.
- `CREWED → SOS` is allowed (a panic is never refused); `CREWED → ABORTED` by ops, the arrival no-show sweep, the client cancel and the crew-assign rollback; `CREWED → COMPLETED` by the drift janitor only.
- The trigger also LOST the historical `DISPATCHED → LIVE` edge the TS machine never allowed and no writer used — the new drift spec surfaced it.

What happens when the scheduled time arrives and…

- **no agency accepted** → the cascade ends `NO_PROVIDER`; nothing was charged; customer stage `no_provider` with the escalate/hotline card. (Unchanged. No automatic fallback — `TODO(LB13)` stands.)
- **agency accepted, no crew** → crew SLA flips `AGENCY_NO_SHOW`, escrow refunded, customer told. NOT re-offered (unchanged, `TODO(LB13)`).
- **crew named (CREWED) but never dispatched** → **NEW deterministic recovery:** the arrival no-show sweep now treats a `CREWED` mission past `arrival_deadline_at` exactly like a `DISPATCHED` one that never arrived — mission ABORTED, crew stood down, booking back to `DISPATCHING`, `offerNext` re-enters the cascade, escrow carried, customer stage `finding_provider`. The deadline is 20 min after the LATER of crew-assign and the scheduled pickup, so a crew named early on a Book Later job is judged from the job's start, not from the moment it was named.
- **agency offline** → in nearest mode the offer-expiry sweep expires its offer (off-duty / stale GPS) and cascades; once CREWED, only the arrival clock applies (above).
- **agency withdraws before crewing** → booking back to `DISPATCHING` with no live offer; visible on the job portal only; relist-timeout closes it `NO_PROVIDER` after 60 min (unchanged, documented gap).
- **customer cancels** → pre-crew free refund; post-crew `cancel_fee_pct` split; a `CREWED` mission is ABORTED (new); LIVE/SOS blocked.
- **still not dispatched at the scheduled time** → nothing fires ON the scheduled instant (unchanged); the arrival clock (20 min from crew-assign) is the recovery. The customer sees `team_assigned` ("Team assigned · not yet dispatched") — truthful, never "dispatched".

## 5. Known gaps, deliberately left (say so before calling it done)

1. **Old apps are served by the old rule.** A key-less `POST /bookings` keeps the one-active guard verbatim (see §2). The trade: an un-updated app cannot hold two bookings — which its UI could not show anyway.
   1b. **Do not raise `hourly_min_hours` above 4 until every installed app carries the duration control.** Older apps always send 4; `estimate()` and `create()` refuse an off-rule duration (never reprice), so a higher minimum bricks their booking flow. The ops-board label says so.
2. **Capacity is still a headcount** (`has_free_cpo_capacity`), not a calendar; a scheduled booking holds no seat until crewed. Unchanged.
3. **No client no-show path, no overrun billing, no auto re-route after AGENCY_NO_SHOW or a withdraw** — all pre-existing (E2E audit 2026-09-03), untouched.
4. **`recon_team`** exists as a service key in the DTO and a label map but is not offered on the service screen and has no lead-time key; "Racon" / "Medical" today are per-hour ADD-ONS, so the duration stepper already scales them.
5. The customer "Client received" and "Service started" are ONE moment (`LIVE`); the copy reads "Protection active · received HH:MMZ". A separate "started" step would be a new business rule and was not invented.
6. The `platform_fee_pct` / `cancel_fee_pct` keys were never admitted into the `service_pricing` CHECK by any earlier migration (an OP-10 latent gap); the new CHECK admits them.
7. **`live_at` is not ONLY the lead's confirmation** (pre-existing): the telemetry fallback in `mission-lead.service.ts` stamps it 15 min after arrival with no tap, and an ops SOS→LIVE resolve stamps it too — including the new `CREWED → SOS → LIVE` path, a billing start with no dispatch and no arrival (ops-only, audited). The `client_received_at` doc comment now says so. Narrowing either is a business-rule change, not made here.
8. **Ops CAN now send a stuck team** — `POST /ops/missions/:id/dispatch` (SUPERVISOR/ADMIN, Idempotency-Key, audits as `OPS`, wakes the whole crew) behind a DISPATCH TEAM button on the mission page when the status is `CREWED`; the board, the SOS bar and the detail page track `CREWED` crews' fixes. A non-auto (legacy) booking stuck at `CREWED` still has no sweep (the arrival sweep is `dispatch_mode = 'auto'` only) — ops dispatches or aborts it. A **deploy-order sentinel** in the drift janitor logs an ERROR every minute naming the migration if `missions.dispatched_at` is missing (never fatal: auth and messaging keep serving).
9. **Client cancel has no deadlock retry** against the opposite-ordered accept transaction (accept locks offer→booking, cancel locks booking→offer); accept retries, cancel can return a raw 500 and a re-tap resolves it. Pre-existing.
10. **`service_window_end_at`** is now shown on the live header ("· UNTIL HH:MMZ") but is not enforced anywhere — no overtime, no auto-complete at window end.
11. The Upcoming chip takes its label from `stage` and its colour from `status` — a `CREWED` row reads "TEAM ASSIGNED" in the CONFIRMED tone. Cosmetic.
